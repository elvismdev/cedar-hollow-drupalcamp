# Storybook image fixtures

The three AVIF files here are crops of one photo, made by running this
site's `16_9_640`, `3_2_400` and `1_1_96` image styles (Focal point scale
and crop at the default centre, then Convert to AVIF) on Drupal 11.4 with
GD.

*   **Source**: [Forest of Himalayan Cedar Tress in Shimla](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Forest_of_Himalayan_Cedar_Tress_in_Shimla.jpg), Wikimedia Commons (page id 175571140, revision of 2025-09-25), the 1280 px wide rendition.
